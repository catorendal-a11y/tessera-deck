# Code of conduct

Tessera is a small project and should be a pleasant place to ask a question, report a bug or send a change.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient. People here have different experience, languages and time zones.
- Criticise the code or the idea, never the person.
- Assume good intent, and say so plainly when something bothers you.
- Keep discussions on the project.

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Repeated off-topic or promotional posts.

## If something goes wrong

Tell the maintainer through GitHub: use **Report content** on the comment, or contact [@catorendal-a11y](https://github.com/catorendal-a11y) directly. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and may block someone who keeps breaking these rules.

This text is short on purpose. Where it is silent, the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) describes the spirit we follow.
